# Changelog

## 0.1.0

- First release: Video ▸ AI Upscaling (RTX Video Super Resolution) turns NVIDIA RTX Video Super Resolution on and off, and remembers the choice across restarts.
- Greyed out with the reason where it cannot work: another system, no NVIDIA RTX GPU, an old driver, software rendering (turn on Video ▸ GPU Rendering (Experimental) and restart), or the player running on the other GPU of a laptop (Windows Settings ▸ System ▸ Display ▸ Graphics).
- A one-time note that Super Resolution must also be on in the NVIDIA app.
- Needs Vestra Player 0.5.16 or later.
