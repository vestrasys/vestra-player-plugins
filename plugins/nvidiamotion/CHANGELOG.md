# Changelog

## 0.2.0

- Three commands choose a mode in one step from the command palette and Ask Vestra: *NVIDIA Motion Smoothing: Off*, *Double Frame Rate (Smoother Video)* and *Match Display (Smoothest Motion)*. They are radio items checked on the saved mode (the window's choice moves the mark too), in the category NVIDIA Motion Smoothing with the window as their place, and outside the menus: Video ▸ NVIDIA Motion Smoothing… stays the only menu item. A mode that cannot work on this computer for the whole run (without an RTX 30 series or newer GPU, a missing driver runtime, or Match Display with software rendering) is greyed out with the player's reason, so on a computer with AMD's GPU Ask Vestra sees that this plugin's modes do not work there. The state is read at start, after a choice and while the window is open; nothing listens while it is closed, so the plugin still holds nothing while off. Translated (Simplified Chinese). The description says the palette and Ask Vestra can choose a mode.

## 0.1.0

- First version: motion smoothing with NVIDIA's frame-rate converter through the player (`plugin.video.motion("nvidia")`, capability `video.motion`): FFmpeg's `fruc_vulkan` on the optical flow hardware of GeForce RTX 30 series and newer GPUs. Video ▸ NVIDIA Motion Smoothing… opens a window with the mode (Off, Double Frame Rate, Match Display), what it does and needs, and a status line with the player's reason when a mode waits or cannot work here. Off by default; the chosen mode is saved and asked for again at each start. Needs Windows with an RTX 30 series or newer GPU and NVIDIA driver 573.38 or newer. A store icon (`assets/icon.png`, 256 × 256): the same ghost trail as AMD Motion Smoothing's, with forward-pointing diamonds in a teal-to-lime gradient. Translations: Simplified Chinese. While the window is open and the converter runs, steady dropped frames (one a second or more over 6 s, and in at least two readings in a row; the first 6 s after the converter starts, restarts (mode change, fallback step) or a new video are not judged, so its start-up burst and one burst from a seek or a resize do not count; read from `player.stats()` every 2 s, only then) show a hint with what helps: with software rendering, that rendering on the CPU cannot keep up and GPU Rendering or a lower resolution helps; with GPU rendering a lower resolution, and Double Frame Rate instead of Match Display when Match Display is the mode.
